“Whereof one cannot speak, thereof one must be silent.”

— Ludwig Wittgenstein, Tractatus Logico-Philosophicus, proposition 7

The closing line of the Tractatus: some things cannot be said in precise language, only shown or lived. Knowing the limit of language, Wittgenstein thought, is part of knowing the world honestly.

Philosophy quote of the day

One quote to learn about, every day.

“Truth does not oppose truth; rather, it accords with it and bears witness to it.”

— Ibn Rushd (Averroes), The Decisive Treatise (Fasl al-Maqal), c. 1179

Averroes — the Andalusian Muslim philosopher who served the Almohad court in Marrakech — argued that reason and revelation are two roads to the same truth: when they seem to clash, the fault lies in our interpretation, not in truth itself. It remains one of history's most influential arguments that philosophy, science, and faith can ultimately agree.

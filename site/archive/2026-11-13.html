“Men are disturbed not by the things which happen, but by the opinions about the things.”

— Epictetus, Enchiridion, V

Events are neutral; it is our judgment about them that wounds or delights us. Between what happens and how you feel sits your own interpretation — and that, unlike the event, is yours to change.
